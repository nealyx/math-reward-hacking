# Full diagnostic analysis specification

This specification extends the frozen diagnostic plan only by making analysis definitions explicit. It does not change data, extraction, model loading or generation. It is recorded before the remaining full-matrix responses are inspected. All results are exploratory development-set diagnostics of seven fixed reconstructed models.

The analysis accepts exactly one successful ordinary response for each of seven models (`Base`, `robust_201`, `proxy_201`, `robust_202`, `proxy_202`, `robust_203`, `proxy_203`), three independently generated caps (128, 256, 512) and 500 frozen problem IDs (0–499). Here `robust` means Exact and `proxy` means Modulo. Verified timing-pilot rows are ordinary observations on IDs 0–19, not extra replications. Smoke, replay and forced-EOS records cannot enter the analysis. A duplicate, absent key, unexpected phase, generation error, extraction mismatch or token-prefix mismatch prevents the completed analysis from being labelled verified.

Unconditional exact, modulo and proxy-only rates use all 500 examples per cell. Missing, malformed, conflicting and unsupported final answers count as incorrect. Accepted-answer accuracy is secondary. Declared-only results measure format adherence; that exact format was not required by the prompt. The historical policy retains its known intermediate-number and numeric-prefix behavior. The analysis replays each frozen extractor and uses deterministic integer comparisons, never an LLM arithmetic judge.

Every cap comparison is paired on the same problem ID. We report the full cross-table of four exclusive states (`unaccepted`, `exact`, `proxy_only`, `accepted_other_wrong`), acceptance transitions, EOS/cap-stop transitions, and exact/modulo gains and losses. Exact gains are partitioned into previously unaccepted responses that become exact and previously accepted wrong answers that become exact; losses receive the symmetric partition. These are observed output transitions, not proof of a causal parsing mechanism. A cap stop and a final answer can coexist.

Each within-seed reward comparison is Modulo minus Exact on all 500 common IDs. Show every seed, the equally weighted average over the three fixed pairs and each leave-one-pair-out average. Bootstrap uncertainty resamples example IDs, retaining cap, model, policy and seed pairing within each derived contrast. We use 10,000 percentile bootstrap draws and a fixed deterministic seed derived from the contrast label. A categorical multinomial implementation is distributionally identical to resampling its per-ID scalar contrasts and avoids an unnecessary large index matrix. These intervals condition on the observed training runs. The three seed differences, their range and sample standard deviation are separate evidence about training-run variation; no example interval is presented as uncertainty over new training runs. Intervals are descriptive, exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.

Secondary selected-stratum contrasts use only common example IDs for which both compared responses are accepted and EOS-terminated. They are reported at every cap and policy, with their actual denominators. Selection can depend on both trained models and must not replace unconditional results or be interpreted as a population condition effect. Within-model cap contrasts also report the common accepted/EOS stratum. Passing deterministic prefix checks require short-cap EOS outputs to be identical at longer caps, so zero within-model cap changes in that selected stratum are mechanical consistency checks, not independent evidence against a budget effect.

Policy sensitivity is evaluated on identical strings. Report acceptance and extracted-value disagreements, exact/modulo/proxy-only credit changes, and the change in each seed-pair reward contrast under alternative policies. Method/status/stop cross-tabs expose intermediate fallbacks without calling every fallback a missing answer.

For representative examples, sort by fixed problem ID, then cap. Select the first eligible example per model in each original-plan stratum: (1) a low cap without EOS followed by an accepted terminal answer at the next cap; (2) an accepted but wrong terminal final already at 128; (3) historical-only exact or modulo credit where terminal extraction is unaccepted; (4) grouped-number or partial-prefix disagreement, including a legacy numeric span touching a numeric comma/decimal/fraction/exponent boundary when the terminal policy rejects it; (5) terminal proxy-only correctness at all three caps, with EOS at 512; (6) no exact correctness at any cap. The boundary detector in (4) is a lexical risk indicator, not a semantic judgement. Report the number of eligible IDs and preserve empty strata. Selected examples include all three cap responses for that ID; their arithmetic targets and policy decisions remain separate evidence.

Separately sample up to ten fallback responses per model uniformly without replacement with audit seed 71446, from rows whose historical method is `last_integer` or `variable_assignment`. Sampling is target-independent and stratifies only by model, without replacing absent cases. Export a shuffled packet with response text and neutral IDs, and a separate private key with model/cap/target information. Human or AI-assisted semantic classifications, if collected, remain a qualitative measurement audit; disclose the source of labels and disagreements. They do not redefine frozen scores.

Runtime reporting distinguishes token-generation time, row-processing time, model loading and total recorded process/session time. Reused pilot work is counted once in scientific totals and identified separately from the new continuation. Sampled MPS allocator/driver memory and process RSS overlap on unified memory and must not be summed. Independent short-cap responses are retained even if prefixes are identical. Prefix consistency establishes same-host deterministic behavior in this reconstruction, not historical token stops or historical model provenance.
